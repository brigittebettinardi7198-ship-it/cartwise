Warehouse clubs

Club gas is often the cheapest in town

Costco, Sam's Club, and BJ's often sell gas well below nearby stations, but you need a membership. Check whether the fuel savings alone cover the annual fee: a 15-gallon weekly fill at 20¢ less per gallon saves about $156 a year.

Fuel rewards

Turn groceries into gas discounts

Kroger Fuel Points and similar grocery rewards take cents off per gallon at participating stations. Rewards usually expire monthly, so use them before they lapse, and fill a full tank to get the most out of each redemption.

FAQ

Where do CartWise prices come from?

Organic grocery prices are national average advertised prices from USDA Agricultural Marketing Service's weekly retail report. Gas prices are AAA state averages. Restaurant estimates use prices from chains' public online menus. Each number shows its source link and retrieval date. CartWise never shows sample or invented prices.

Is CartWise free?

Yes, and you don't need an account.

What is a unit-price trap?

It's when the bigger package costs more per ounce, or when shelf tags use different units. Compare the price per the same unit.

When do stores mark down meat and bread?

Meat is often reduced near its sell-by date, usually in the morning. Bakery items are often reduced late in the day. Ask your store's departments, since timing varies.

What is shrinkflation?

It's when the package shrinks while the price holds or rises. Check the net weight and the unit price.

Can I stack coupons with cash-back apps?

Usually: sale price + store digital coupon + manufacturer coupon (if allowed) + cash-back app rebate.

What do the organic benchmark prices mean?

They're USDA's national weighted-average advertised (sale and circular) prices for organic produce at major US grocery chains for the latest week. Use them as a benchmark. If your store charges much more, look at store brands, Aldi, or Walmart.

Should I buy everything organic?

Not necessarily. Prioritize organic for items on the EWG Dirty Dozen list, buy Clean Fifteen produce conventionally, and use frozen and store-brand organic to save.

How does the restaurant estimator work?

It takes median real menu prices by category (entrées, kids' meals, drinks, appetizers, desserts), multiplies them by your party size and choices, then adds sales tax and tip (tip on the pre-tax subtotal). The result is an estimate, because prices vary by location.

Are the gas prices real?

Yes. They're AAA state averages, shown with the date retrieved. Individual station prices appear only when shoppers report them.
