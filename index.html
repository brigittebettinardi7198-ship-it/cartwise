FAQ

Where do CartWise prices come from?

Organic grocery prices are national average advertised prices from USDA Agricultural Marketing Service's weekly retail report. Gas prices are AAA state averages. Restaurants are real nearby places from OpenStreetMap. Each number shows its source link and retrieval date. CartWise never shows sample or invented prices.

Is CartWise free?

Yes, and you don't need an account.

What is a unit-price trap?

It's when the bigger package costs more per ounce, or when shelf tags use different units. Compare the price per the same unit.

When do stores mark down meat and bread?

Meat is often reduced near its sell-by date, usually in the morning. Bakery items are often reduced late in the day. Ask your store's departments, since timing varies.

What is shrinkflation?

It's when the package shrinks while the price holds or rises. Check the net weight and the unit price.

Can I stack coupons with cash-back apps?

Usually: sale price + store digital coupon + manufacturer coupon (if allowed) + cash-back app rebate.

What do the organic benchmark prices mean?

They're USDA's national weighted-average advertised (sale and circular) prices for organic produce at major US grocery chains for the latest week. Use them as a benchmark. If your store charges much more, look at store brands, Aldi, or Walmart.

Can I compare organic prices between stores?

Yes. Compare stores shows real organic produce prices from Trader Joe's and Whole Foods public websites next to the USDA benchmark, with the cheapest highlighted when sizes are comparable. Target and Costco didn't publish organic produce prices we could verify. Sprouts, Kroger, Walmart, Aldi, Safeway and Publix haven't been checked yet.

How does "Restaurants near you" work?

Your ZIP is turned into a map location with the free Zippopotam.us service. In Chicago and the western/southwest suburbs (for example Oak Brook, La Grange, Lemont, Homer Glen, Orland Park, Tinley Park, Downers Grove, Elmhurst), restaurants with real menu prices copied from their own menu pages are listed first, cheapest first, and you can set a max per person. Each has a source link and date. The typical meal (entrée plus drink, or entrée only when no drink price is published) is computed by CartWise. Other nearby places come from OpenStreetMap without prices. CartWise never invents prices or ratings.

Why are there no fast-food prices?

Major fast-food chains only show menu prices after you choose a store, so there's no verifiable national price to publish. CartWise doesn't invent numbers.

Should I buy everything organic?

Not necessarily. Prioritize organic for items on the EWG Dirty Dozen list, buy Clean Fifteen produce conventionally, and use frozen and store-brand organic to save.

Are the gas prices real?

Yes. They're AAA state and national averages for regular, mid-grade, premium and diesel, shown with the date retrieved.
